EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - SAMPLE POLICY EXCERPTS

PAID TIME OFF
Full-time employees receive twenty days of paid time off per calendar year. Submit planned leave through the employee portal at least five business days in advance. Unused days do not roll over into the next calendar year.

PARENTAL LEAVE
Eligible employees may take twelve weeks of paid parental leave after the birth or adoption of a child. Notify the People team at least thirty days before planned leave when possible. Benefits continue during approved parental leave.

TRAVEL EXPENSES
Submit itemized travel receipts through the expense portal within thirty days after returning from a trip. Manager approval is required for any single expense above 250 dollars. Meals are reimbursed up to 65 dollars per travel day.

SECURITY INCIDENTS
Report suspected security incidents to the security team within 24 hours of discovery. Do not investigate a suspected breach or delete potentially relevant messages. Use the incident reporting channel and include the approximate time, affected system, and a short description.
